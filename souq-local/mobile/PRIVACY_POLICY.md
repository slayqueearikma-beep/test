# MarGem Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 2026

MarGem ("we", "our", "the app") is a marketplace that connects buyers with local businesses in Morocco.

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** email address, display name, and account type (buyer or seller).
- **Seller information:** business name, description, address, city, phone number, location coordinates, and images you upload.
- **Usage data:** app interactions, device type, and crash logs (if enabled).
- **Location:** only when you grant permission, to show nearby businesses on the map.

## How we use information

- To create and manage your account.
- To display businesses, products, and reviews.
- To host images you upload.
- To improve app reliability and security.

## Data storage

- Account data is stored in our secure PostgreSQL database.
- Images are stored in Azure Blob Storage.
- Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers required to run the app (e.g. cloud hosting).

## Your rights

You may request account deletion by contacting us at **privacy@margem.app**.
